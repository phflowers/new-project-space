# Fountain Health

**Badge:** Paul H. Flowers Jr. — Regional Business Development Consultant
**Card domain:** fountainhealth.com

## What I was hired to do

Business development for a healthcare offering aimed at employers and
their members — pitched as a different model than traditional carrier
plans.

## What the role gave me access to

- How a "disruptor" pitch is constructed for the employer market and
  what survives contact with carrier networks and PBMs.
- The economics behind "preventive-first" or "value-based" framing
  when the underlying claims still flow through the same rails.
- The gap between the marketing deck and the operations meeting.

## What I will and won't write about

- Will: the public-facing claims and the documented model.
- Won't: internal financials, partner names, or unpublished pilots.

## Why this one mattered for the turn

This is the role where the pattern across the other three stopped
looking like coincidence and started looking like an industry. That's
the moment the writing project began.
