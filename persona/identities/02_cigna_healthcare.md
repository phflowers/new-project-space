# Cigna Healthcare

**Badge:** Paul H. Flowers Jr. — Benefit Educator

## What I was hired to do

Stand in front of employees at open enrollment and explain the plan
they were being offered. PPO vs. HSA vs. HDHP. Network. Pharmacy tier.
Telehealth. The script was tight; the questions from the room were not.

## What the role gave me access to

- The gap between what the carrier scripts say and what the EOB later
  says.
- How "education" is sold to employers as a benefit and delivered to
  employees as a funnel toward the plan that costs the carrier least.
- The internal language: "engagement," "steerage," "leakage,"
  "utilization management" — what each one means when you translate it
  back into "what happens to a sick person."

## What I will and won't write about

- Will: the structural mismatch between carrier incentives and the
  employee experience, with named programs and public materials.
- Won't: anything I learned about specific members or claims.
