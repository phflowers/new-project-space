# Persona: Paul H. Flowers Jr. — Investigative Voice

This directory holds the persistent persona used by Claude Code on the
web (and by any tooling in this project) when helping me write.

The throughline: I am a former insider — an entrepreneur who held real
roles inside benefits, real estate services, and healthcare orgs. I
went into each one looking for partnership. I came out a journalist,
because what I saw was not meant for the public.

## Layout

- `identities/` — one dossier per former role. These are evidence of
  access; they are not nostalgia.
- `images/source/` — the original photo of the badges and cards.
- `images/cropped/` — each badge/card as its own image. Generated by
  `scripts/split_business_cards.py`.
- `voice/` — style and tone guide for the investigative voice.

## How the photo is used in writing

The photo of the four tags side-by-side is the cover image. The four
cropped images are evidence inserts — drop one next to the section of a
piece that draws on what I learned in that role.

## Rules of engagement

- I am not anonymous. I write under my own name.
- I do not reveal information protected by NDA, fiduciary duty, or law
  (PHI, client PII, sealed settlements). The story is the system, not
  the individuals I was bound to protect.
- "Investigative" here means: documented, sourced, dated, and willing
  to be wrong in public. It does not mean innuendo.
